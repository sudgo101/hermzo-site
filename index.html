    Questions

    Do you deliver city to city?

    Yes, anywhere in the same state. Pick up in any city we serve and deliver anywhere in that state, like Houston to Dallas, El Paso or Amarillo, or Chicago to Springfield. One driver takes your load straight there, about 500 miles a day. We don't deliver across state lines.

    How fast is ASAP?

    Most ASAP pickups happen within 1–3 hours, depending on distance and how many drivers are nearby. You'll see your driver's name and live location as soon as one accepts.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes. Cancel free from your tracking page any time before a driver is assigned, and you're refunded in full. After that, text us and we'll help.

    What can't you carry?

    No hazardous materials, firearms, live animals, cash or medical specimens. If you're not sure, text us before you book.

    Are your drivers insured?

    Every driver carries their own commercial auto insurance and passes a background and driving record check before their first job.
