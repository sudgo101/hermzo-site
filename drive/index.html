Hermzo Driver

Your vehicle. Your jobs. Paid every delivery.

Cargo vans, box trucks, pickups with trailers and cars all earn on Hermzo. Go online when you want, accept the jobs you like, and skip the ones you don't.

  • Keep 75% of every job, plus half of ASAP and same-day surcharges
  • 100% of tips are yours
  • See the pay, distance and load before you accept
  • Claim scheduled jobs days ahead to plan your week

The Hermzo Driver app is coming to the App Store and Google Play. Email support@hermzo.com with your city and vehicle to get early access.

What you need

Your vehicle

A car or SUV, cargo van, pickup with a trailer, or a box truck (liftgate is a plus). Under 26,001 lbs, so no CDL needed.

Your insurance

Commercial auto insurance in your name. You'll upload your card in the app.

A clean record

A valid driver's license, plus a background check and driving record check before your first job.

Driver questions

Am I an employee?

No. Drivers are independent contractors. You choose when to drive and which jobs to take.

How do I get paid?

Add your bank in the app. Your pay for each job is sent to your account after you mark it delivered.

What if I need to drop a job?

You can, but dropping jobs at the last minute lowers your reliability score, and repeated last-minute drops can deactivate your account.
